Welcome to St. Wilfred's ACS College Mira Road

St. Wilfred's ACS College Mira Road is a premier institution dedicated to providing quality commerce education in Mumbai. Located in the heart of Mira Road, we offer comprehensive programs that prepare students for successful careers in commerce and business.

Our college combines academic excellence with practical learning, ensuring students gain both theoretical knowledge and real-world skills. With experienced faculty and modern infrastructure, we create an environment conducive to learning and growth.

We focus on holistic development through various co-curricular activities, industry interactions, and skill development programs that enhance employability and entrepreneurial capabilities.

For more information about our programs and admissions, visit our main website at stwilfredsacs.com

    📖 Programme Overview

    The Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) at St. Wilfred's College is a multidisciplinary undergraduate programme designed to cultivate critical thinking, cultural awareness, creativity, communication, and analytical abilities. Grounded in the liberal arts tradition and aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, this course allows students to explore multiple disciplines while developing a strong foundation in chosen specializations like Psychology, Geography, Political Science, and History Majors.

    Additional subjects include Economics, Sociology, Hindi, English Literature, and Philosophy.

    ✅ Eligibility

    • Minimum Qualification: 10+2 or equivalent (H.S.C. from any recognized board)
    • Stream: Open to students from Arts, Commerce, or Science background
    • Admissions: Based on merit and reservation norms as per the University of Mumbai

    🗓️ Duration & Structure (As per NEP 2020 Guidelines)

    Standard 3 Years (6 Semesters)
    Honours 4 Years (8 Semesters)
    Exit Options
    After 1 Year Certificate in Arts
    After 2 Years Diploma in Arts
    After 3 Years Bachelor's Degree
    After 4 Years Honours with Specialization / Research

    📚 Curriculum Highlights

    • Core and Discipline-Specific Courses (e.g., History, Sociology, Psychology)
    • Skill Enhancement Courses (e.g., Communication, Critical Thinking, Writing Skills)
    • Value-Added Courses (e.g., Environmental Studies, Indian Knowledge System)
    • Multilingual Studies (including regional and global languages)
    • Research, Internships, and Community Engagement Projects (from Year 2 onwards)

    ⭐ Salient Features

    • Holistic Development: Emphasis on personality development, ethics, and civic responsibility
    • Flexible Learning Pathways: Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)
    • Skill Integration: Soft skills, research aptitude, digital literacy
    • Experienced Faculty: A mix of academic and industry-experienced educators
    • Career & Progression Support: UPSC/MPSC coaching, career counselling, competitive exam prep

    💼 Career Prospects

    Graduates of B.A. from St. Wilfred's College are well-positioned for careers in:

    🏛️ Civil Services & Government Jobs
    🏫 Education & Research
    📡 Media & Communication
    🤝 NGOs & Social Work
    🏢 Corporate Sectors (HR, PR, CSR)
    🎓 Higher Education (M.A., MSW, MBA, Law)
    🌍 Relevance in Today's Competitive World

    A Degree That's Both Timeless & Future-Ready

    The B.A. degree nurtures analytical and people-centric skills increasingly valued across diverse sectors. In a world driven by rapid change and socio-political dynamics, Arts graduates are equipped to think deeply, act ethically, and lead responsibly — making this programme both timeless and future-ready.

    📖 Programme Overview

    The Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) at St. Wilfred's College is a well-structured, industry-relevant undergraduate programme focused on building strong foundations in accounting, finance, taxation, business law, and economics. The course is designed in alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, offering flexibility, skill-based learning, and multidisciplinary exposure to meet the demands of the global economy.

    It prepares students for professional courses (CA, CS, CMA, CFA), competitive exams, entrepreneurship, and careers in commerce and corporate sectors.

    ✅ Eligibility

    • Qualification: Passed Class 12 (H.S.C.) or equivalent from a recognized board
    • Stream Preference: Commerce preferred, but students from Arts and Science streams are also eligible
    • Admission Basis: Merit-based selection as per University of Mumbai norms and reservation policy

    🗓️ Programme Duration & Exit Options (As per NEP 2020)

    Standard 3 Years (6 Semesters)
    Honours 4 Years (8 Semesters)
    Multiple Exit Options
    After 1 Year Certificate in Commerce
    After 2 Years Diploma in Commerce
    After 3 Years B.Com Degree
    After 4 Years B.Com (Hons.) with Specialization / Project

    📚 Curriculum Structure

    • Core Subjects: Financial Accounting, Business Communication, Business Economics, Taxation, Auditing, Management
    • Ability Enhancement Courses: Communication Skills, Environmental Studies, IT Tools
    • Skill-Based & Interdisciplinary Courses: E-Commerce, Entrepreneurship, Business Analytics
    • Research / Internship (Honours Year): Practical exposure and academic research project

    ⭐ Salient Features

    • Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)
    • Internships & Practical Training through tie-ups with industry and financial institutions
    • Entrepreneurship Support via workshops, Udyog Melas, and incubation guidance
    • Add-on Certifications in GST, Tally, Excel, and Business Communication
    • Guest Lectures and industry interactions with CA, CS, Bankers, Entrepreneurs

    💼 Weightage in Today's Competitive World

    The B.Com programme is one of the most in-demand degrees globally, offering academic flexibility and career versatility. Graduates are equipped for:

    📜 Professional Certifications — CA, CS, CFA, MBA
    🏢 Corporate Roles — Accounting, Finance, Banking, HR, Operations
    🏛️ Public Sector Exams — UPSC, MPSC, SSC, Banking Exams
    🚀 Entrepreneurship — Skill-based & Digital Business Learning
    💻 E-Commerce & Business Analytics
    📊 Finance & Investment Sectors
    🌍 Career & Future Readiness

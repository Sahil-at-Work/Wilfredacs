Events in Wilfred:
1. Garba Nights: <img width="448" height="287" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/379f0979-babb-4d87-9dcb-37072b48aab7" /> GARBA
Moving ahead, let us take a moment to celebrate the colours, culture and traditions of India through one of our most vibrant celebrations – Garba.
Garba is more than just a dance; it represents our rich cultural heritage, unity and togetherness.
At St. Wilfred’s College, the Garba celebration brought students together in an atmosphere filled with music, energy, enthusiasm and festive spirit.
Students got an opportunity to celebrate Indian traditions, participate in cultural activities and create unforgettable memories with their friends and faculty members.
Such celebrations help students stay connected to our culture while encouraging confidence, participation and a sense of belonging.
At our college, we believe that education is not only about academics but also about experiencing different cultures and celebrating together.
Because traditions connect us to our roots, while celebrations bring us closer together!
